The 5th edition : 15 - 17 of April

Devoxx Greece 2027

Devoxx Greece 2027, the fourth edition, will take place from April 15 to 17 at the Athens Megaron International Conference Center.

Devoxx Greece is the evolution of the Voxxed Days Athens conference, and it has become a 3-day conference where developer communities come together to explore the latest technology advancements with some of the most inspiring speakers in our sector.

The Devoxx family (Belgium, France, Poland, Marocco, and Ukraine) welcomes annually over 15.000 Devoxxians!  

Diverse local and global talent introduce the latest and most vital content from the development world, with a range of sessions covering Java, Cloud, Big Data, Security, Architecture, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Robotics, Programming Languages, Methodologies, and Developer Culture.

Devoxx Greece expands your knowledge base, sharpens your skills, and provides hands-on experience with the latest technologies.
