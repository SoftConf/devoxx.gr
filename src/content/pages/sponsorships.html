Sponsorships

Each year we welcome amazing companies who want to show their support for the developer community.

Sponsorships come in five main forms: Diamond, Platinum, Gold, Silver and Bronze, with a maximum number of slots available at each level. We also offer premium bolt-ons for some sponsorship levels, and a range of à la carte options for sponsors at all levels. Sponsors are provided with a shell-scheme booth space, info desks, and stools. Sponsors can choose to brand the shell scheme directly, bring their own pop-up booth, or create a custom-built booth within the space provided. Our sponsors choose their position on the exhibition floor, with space allocated on a first-come, first-served basis. Therefore, the first company to sign at the Gold level gets first choice of a Gold booth position.

Become a sponsor

If your company would like to support Devoxx Greece, or you want to find out more, drop an email to pat@devoxx.gr to send you back the detailed sponsoring document. 

When a sponsorship is agreed upon, we’ll prepare a contract for signing.

Once confirmed, we’ll announce your presence at the event, provide all the info you need, and set-up calls to guide your team through all of the next steps.
