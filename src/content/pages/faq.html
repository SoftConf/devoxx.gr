The different types of sessions - Speaker passes

Speakers receive a 3-day combo pass when one (or more) of the following session types is accepted:

  • Conference talks are 40 minutes and take place on Thu/Fri/Sat (*)
  • Deep Dive talks are in-depth presentations of 3 hours taking place on Fri/Sat (= 75m + 30m break + 75m) (*)
  • Hands-on Labs are 2-hour sessions on Friday and Saturday – bring your own portable device!

(*) Recorded

Schedule

  • We serve breakfast from 9h00 onwards, complimentary for all attendees.
  • The Conference days start on Thursday morning with a keynote at 10h00
  • Friday evening, join us for the event after-party.
  • The last Conference sessions end on Saturday at ~15h00

Do I need to register for the sessions?

No, we use the first-come, first-served algorithm.

Badge Pickup

Pick up your Conference badge on Wednesday between 7:00 and 8:00 p.m., or on Thursday morning from 8:00 onwards. IMPORTANT NOTE: Badges are strictly personal and can’t be shared.  When you "forget your badge", you'll be asked to return home and retrieve it.
